# TLDR

**Motto: Relay the information to me with less reading.**

Keep the same substance and make it easier to absorb. Optimize for the reader's effort, not merely word count.

## Choose the content

- With no argument, condense the preceding assistant answer. Use conversation context to preserve what it means.
- With supplied text or a named topic, condense that content.
- When invoked alongside a task, complete the task with the same care and scope, then apply this skill to the user-facing explanation. Brevity does not reduce the work or verification required.

## Compress without losing meaning

- Lead with the answer, outcome, or decision. Keep the reasons and next action needed to understand or use it.
- Preserve distinct findings, material caveats, uncertainty, tradeoffs, and unresolved questions. Keep numbers, units, names, commands, and references exact when they matter. Do not turn an estimate into a fact or partial success into completion.
- Merge repetition, group parallel details, and cut throat-clearing, generic background, process narration, and repeated conclusions. Replace lengthy examples with the smallest useful one.
- Use familiar words and short, complete sentences. Avoid cryptic fragments, dense abbreviations, or symbol-heavy shorthand that takes longer to decode.
- Choose the shortest clear form: a paragraph, a few bullets, or a small comparison table. Do not force a template or an arbitrary word limit. Already concise content may need little change.

## Let visuals or analogies replace prose

- Use a graph or diagram when it makes a relationship, trend, flow, or comparison faster to grasp. Keep labels readable and show only supported data. Skip decorative visuals.
- Use one brief, familiar analogy when it explains the mechanism in fewer words. Make the mapping clear and retain any limitation needed to avoid a misleading impression.
- Let the visual or analogy carry the explanation. Do not repeat it in a full prose version.

Return the condensed content directly, without announcing the rewrite or appending an offer to explain more. Before sending, check that a reader can reach the same conclusion and take the same action without needing the longer version.
