# Post-mortem

End with three short points, in this order:

- **Issue:** What was wrong or needed to change, and the main reason why.
- **Necessary change:** The minimum change that addressed it, and why it worked.
- **Could omit:** Work that could have been skipped while still achieving the result; say “Nothing identified” if none.

Use the conversation and relevant changes as evidence. Distinguish confirmed causes from guesses. Identify optional work without undoing it. Skip process narration and exhaustive change lists; aim for one sentence per point.
