# SIA — Image Brief Template

This file is the canonical visual style brief. Read it whenever generating an image brief for a post.

The output is a Gemini-ready text prompt the user can paste directly into Google Gemini's image generator. SIA never generates the image itself.

---

## The Sensory-Minds visual language

All Sensory-Minds LinkedIn images share a visual language built on **conceptual editorial photography**. They are visual metaphors for the post's idea, not literal illustrations. No people. No products. No corporate stock energy. No text in the image except the SENSORYMINDS wordmark at the top centre.

The visual language has **two modes**. Both are valid. Pick the one that fits the post's mood.

### Mode 1: Sculptural studio
Best for: sharper opinion posts, structural takes, posts about technology, systems, or transformation. The serious side.

- **Background**: dark, moody. Deep blue, near-black, scratched metal, wet asphalt at dusk.
- **Lighting**: dramatic studio lighting. Strong directional light, intentional shadows, warm rim light.
- **Objects**: sculptural, three-dimensional, often rendered or shot in a studio. Materials: terracotta clay, polished steel, copper wire, clear acrylic, glass prisms, smooth pastel-coloured ceramic forms.
- **Composition**: top-down or carefully composed studio angle. Subject(s) arranged with intent. Often a sequence of objects showing transformation, or a single sculptural form catching light.
- **Accent palette**: terracotta, copper, warm metallic, rose, blush against the cool dark base.
- **Reference posts**: clay-to-mould sequence (iteration / forming), abstract objects at a road crossroad (decision points), glass prisms on wet asphalt (clarity on the path).

### Mode 2: Paper craft
Best for: lighter observational posts, posts about ideas / process / thinking, posts where the metaphor is best expressed through something hand-made and quiet.

- **Background**: soft pastel. Powder blue, off-white, warm cream. Paper texture visible.
- **Lighting**: soft, diffused, daylight. Gentle shadows.
- **Objects**: hand-made paper, cardboard, folded card, post-it stacks. Hand-drawn marks in pencil or marker. Small saturated accent colour — a yellow crayon scribble, a green felt-tip stroke, a red dot, a holographic shimmer.
- **Composition**: minimal. Lots of negative space. Sometimes a single subject; sometimes a few objects in a deliberate grid.
- **Accent palette**: one or two saturated highlight colours per image (yellow, green, red, holographic rainbow) against the pastel base.
- **Reference posts**: crumpled paper among neat post-it stacks (the outlier), folded book and cardboard stack with a green scribble (research, notes, process), a line with a tally count (measurement).

### How to choose between the modes
- Tone of post: heavier / structural → sculptural studio. Lighter / observational / process-driven → paper craft.
- When in doubt, default to sculptural studio for opinion posts about AI and technology, paper craft for posts about thinking, design process, or quieter shifts.
- For Company Moments: usually skip both modes and use a photo the user supplies (a team photo, the actual award, the actual podcast cover). Generate an image brief only if the user explicitly asks for one.

## Image specifications (always include in every brief)

- **Aspect ratio**: 1:1 square.
- **Resolution**: 1024 × 1024 minimum (Gemini default is fine).
- **SENSORYMINDS wordmark**: top centre, in white, condensed sans-serif. "SENSORY" in light/thin weight, "MINDS" in bold weight. Approximately 8–10% of image height.
- **No other text** anywhere in the image.
- **No watermarks, no other logos, no signatures.**
- **No people, no faces, no recognisable products, no copyrighted IP.**

## The image brief template

Use this exact structure when writing the brief. Adapt the content; keep the headings.

```
Concept
[One sentence describing the visual metaphor and what idea from the post it represents. Be specific about the object(s), arrangement, and what they stand for.]

Visual mode
[Sculptural studio | Paper craft]

Scene
[2–4 sentences describing what is in the frame, materials, arrangement, surface, background. Concrete. Visual.]

Lighting and mood
[1–2 sentences. Direction of light, hardness/softness, overall mood word (moody, quiet, dramatic, contemplative).]

Colour palette
[Base colours of the background and surface, plus the 1–2 accent colours. Be specific — "terracotta", "deep navy", "powder blue", "holographic rainbow shimmer".]

Composition and framing
[Top-down / studio angle / eye-level / close-up. Where the subject sits in the frame. Negative space.]

Brand and text
SENSORYMINDS wordmark top centre, white, condensed sans-serif. "SENSORY" thin weight, "MINDS" bold weight. No other text or logos anywhere in the image.

Technical
1:1 square aspect ratio. 1024 × 1024 or higher. Photorealistic editorial photography style, not illustration. Sharp focus on the subject. No people, no faces, no products, no copyrighted material.

Negative prompt
text overlays, captions, watermarks, additional logos, stock photo people, smiling office workers, generic tech imagery, glowing blue circuits, brain graphics, cartoon style, illustration, painterly style
```

## Worked examples

### Example brief 1 — for an opinion post about AI replacing routine work

```
Concept
Three identical clay vessels in different states — one raw and unformed, one shaped by hand around a wire armature, one finished and polished in a metal mould. The metaphor: routine work being replaced isn't the loss it sounds like; it's the surfacing of what was always the actual craft.

Visual mode
Sculptural studio

Scene
Three terracotta forms arranged in a row on dark scratched metal. Left: a rough, unformed lump of clay with visible thumbprints. Middle: the same clay shape held inside a thin steel wire cage that defines its form. Right: a smooth, polished version of the shape sitting inside a precision-machined steel mould. The objects sit at equal spacing, evenly lit from above.

Lighting and mood
Dramatic overhead studio lighting with strong directional shadows. Moody, considered, slightly industrial. Warm light catching the terracotta edges.

Colour palette
Background: near-black with dark scratched metallic surface. Subject: terracotta and warm clay tones. Accents: brushed steel grey on the wire cage and mould.

Composition and framing
Top-down view. Three subjects evenly spaced across the horizontal midline. Generous negative space above and below.

Brand and text
SENSORYMINDS wordmark top centre, white, condensed sans-serif. "SENSORY" thin weight, "MINDS" bold weight. No other text or logos anywhere in the image.

Technical
1:1 square aspect ratio. 1024 × 1024 or higher. Photorealistic editorial photography style, not illustration. Sharp focus across all three subjects. No people, no faces, no products, no copyrighted material.

Negative prompt
text overlays, captions, watermarks, additional logos, stock photo people, smiling office workers, generic tech imagery, glowing blue circuits, brain graphics, cartoon style, illustration, painterly style
```

### Example brief 2 — for an observational post about design teams adapting

```
Concept
A neat grid of identical pale-blue post-it pads, with one in the centre crumpled into a small folded paper sculpture. The metaphor: the team member who refused to follow the template is the one producing the work that lasts.

Visual mode
Paper craft

Scene
Roughly fifteen pale-blue post-it pads arranged in a loose grid on a soft cream paper surface. Each pad is square, stacked a few millimetres thick. In the middle of the grid, one pad is missing — replaced by the same pad crumpled and folded into an angular paper sculpture. A subtle holographic rainbow shimmer plays across two or three of the pads at the edges.

Lighting and mood
Soft diffused daylight from the upper left. Quiet, contemplative, almost still-life. Gentle shadows under each pad.

Colour palette
Background: warm cream and off-white. Subject: pale powder blue post-it pads. Accent: holographic rainbow shimmer on a small number of pads.

Composition and framing
Top-down view, slight tilt. Grid fills most of the frame. The crumpled centre pad is the clear visual anchor.

Brand and text
SENSORYMINDS wordmark top centre, white, condensed sans-serif. "SENSORY" thin weight, "MINDS" bold weight. No other text or logos anywhere in the image.

Technical
1:1 square aspect ratio. 1024 × 1024 or higher. Photorealistic editorial photography style, not illustration. Sharp focus on the centre crumpled pad. No people, no faces, no products, no copyrighted material.

Negative prompt
text overlays, captions, watermarks, additional logos, stock photo people, smiling office workers, generic tech imagery, glowing blue circuits, brain graphics, cartoon style, illustration, painterly style
```

## Things to avoid in image briefs

- Literal interpretations of the post topic ("a robot helping a designer" → no).
- Generic tech imagery: glowing circuits, abstract neural nets, brain illustrations, code on screens, hands shaking with holograms.
- People, especially smiling people in business attire.
- Products: laptops, phones, devices, branded objects.
- Overused metaphors: chess pieces, lightbulbs, gears, mountains being climbed, finish-line tape.
- Anything that looks like a stock photo.

The image should feel like a still from a brand campaign, not an illustration of an idea.
